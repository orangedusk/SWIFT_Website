Common questions

Do I need an appointment? +

No. For urgent illness or injury you can walk in any time we're open. Planned treatments, like iron infusions or specialist follow-ups, are by appointment.

What ages does SWIFT treat? +

Patients aged 3 months and up, from minor injuries to non-life-threatening illness.

How is this different from a hospital ED? +

SWIFT is an emergency-physician-led urgent care clinic for non-life-threatening cases, built to be faster to access than a hospital ED. For life-threatening emergencies, always call 000.
