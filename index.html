Where to go depends on how serious it is. Search a symptom, or scan the three columns below.

Life-threatening, or getting worse fast

  • Chest pain, pressure or tightness
  • Severe trouble breathing, or choking
  • Signs of stroke: face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech
  • Unconscious, collapsed, or having a seizure
  • Heavy bleeding that won't stop
  • Severe allergic reaction, with swelling of the face or throat
  • Major injury, like a car crash or a fall from height
  • Thoughts of harming yourself or others

Urgent, but not life-threatening

  • Sprains and strains
  • Suspected broken bones
  • Cuts that may need stitches
  • Minor burns
  • Fevers, including children over 3 months
  • Vomiting, diarrhoea or dehydration
  • Ear, eye, throat or urinary infections
  • Mild asthma, rashes or allergic reactions
  • Back pain or a sports injury

Common questions

Do I need an appointment? +

No. For urgent illness or injury you can walk in any time we're open. Planned treatments, like iron infusions or specialist follow-ups, are by appointment.

What ages does SWIFT treat? +

Patients aged 3 months and up, from minor injuries to non-life-threatening illness.

How is this different from a hospital ED? +

SWIFT is an emergency-physician-led urgent care clinic for non-life-threatening cases, built to be faster to access than a hospital ED. For life-threatening emergencies, always call 000.
